How This Works

This app exists to put your imagination before your sound — so that you're working on what you want to hear rather than how to produce it.

Imagine — what you want

Imagine is the imaginal channel. A character, a circumstance, an image, a way of meaning it. It's the target: the thing you're trying to hear before you play a note.

"As if someone just walked in" · "Silk over gravel" · "A liar telling the truth"

Perceive — where you stand

Perceive is the perceptual channel. A sensation or a sound you can actually put your attention on. Every item notices something; none of them instruct you to do something. That's deliberate — technical self-correction is the thing that gets you in your own way.

"Sounds that are not you" · "Where you feel the sound in your body" · "The sound as it arrives, not as you planned it"

Why they feel like the same thing

Because they nearly are. Both are places to put your attention — they just sit in different rooms. The useful way to hold them:

Imagine is what you want.
Perceive is where you stand while getting it.

Imagine is the destination; Perceive is the vantage point you watch it from. That's why Perceive never needs to describe the sound — Imagine already did.

The two modes

Imagine + Perceive gives you both at once. The work is reconciling them — holding an imagined intention while your attention rests somewhere specific. Harder, and worth it.

One at a time gives you one or the other. A single clear intention with nothing competing for it. Use it when holding both turns into thinking about both.

Neither is the correct one. They're different practices.

Using it

Take the first reading that arrives. The prompts are provocations, not instructions — if one seems wrong for the material, that friction is usually the interesting part. Anything that consistently doesn't work for you can be deleted in Edit Parameters.
