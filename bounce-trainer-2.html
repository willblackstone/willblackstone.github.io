Listen experimental

The microphone finds the start of each note and shows how it lined up with the landing. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. The tock is muted while listening. If the pulse is on, use headphones so the mic doesn't hear the clicks.

Play your note the instant the ball lands.

  1. Watch it fly. The chute aims, the ball launches, and gravity brings it down.
  2. Hear it first. Have the note in your ear before the ball lands.
  3. Land with it. The floor flashes red at the moment of impact. That's where your note goes.

It's not a reaction test. There's always time to see it coming. You're starting on level 1: half speed, the same arc each time, with the landing spot marked.

How it works

A ball launches from the chute, flies, and lands. Play your note the instant it hits the floor. The floor flashes red at each moment you should play.

Getting going

  • Press Start, or Space, or tap the stage.
  • Work through the levels with ‹ and ›. Each adds one new thing. Move on when the landings feel unhurried, not when you get them all.
  • Under each level is something to notice while you play.

Things to try

  • Land and hold: the ball lands, rolls and drops into a hole. Hold the note until it disappears.
  • On a pulse: every landing falls on a beat, so the ball and the metronome arrive together.
  • Hear it first: the note sounds while the chute aims, then you carry it through the flight in your head.
  • Listen: lets the microphone show how early or late each note started. Calibrate once per device with the speakers on.
