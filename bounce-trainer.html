Chute angle

Most launches come from the top of the range, with the odd lower one.

Launch power

While the chute is hidden, angle and power are set for each launch automatically, so the ball comes in from the left, rises high and lands anywhere across the floor.

Bounciness

1 is a dead ball, 10 is a superball. Lower values make the bounces speed up and die away sooner.

Notes

Only the bounces you should play flash red. Once you've played them, the ball turns grey.
